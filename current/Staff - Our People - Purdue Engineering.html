Purdue Engineering Staff

Staff in the College of Engineering make their mark every day. Whether they are supporting faculty in their teaching and researching endeavors, working with students as they progress in their studies, or taking care of the buildings and facilities within which we work, each staff member plays a key role in the operations of the College and the University.
